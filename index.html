About Me

Design backed by psychology, not just instinct.

Most websites fail before anyone reads a word. Not because of bad copy or wrong colors — because nobody asked how a human brain actually decides to act. I have been obsessed with that question ever since.

I'm a UX/UI and product designer based in Agadir, Morocco. I co-founded Drawever, an AI creative platform, leading all design from day one. By sitting closely with the team and listening to real users, we grew to 50,000 creators — with a 39.6% bounce rate and a 22% lift in conversions.

The tool is never the point — the thinking behind it is. I work in Figma and build in Framer, but I adapt to whatever a project calls for. That is what problem-solvers do.
